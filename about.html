About

Practical digital solutions for growing businesses.

Rebahumi helps small, growing and emerging businesses use technology to simplify their operations, improve how they present themselves and connect with their customers.

We believe every business deserves access to practical digital solutions. Going digital doesn't need to be complicated, so we build things that fit the way your business actually works.

We currently work around Kwa-Thema, Tsakane and Duduza.

Why Rebahumi exists

The informal sector shouldn't be left behind

Rebahumi was started by Sinothando Mthingi, an aspiring economist. As the world moves towards being digital, it's important that the informal sector moves with it.

Informal businesses make a big contribution to economic growth. But many of them work with limited technology, infrastructure and resources, which keeps productivity low.

Rebahumi is our way of helping change that. We bring simple, practical digital tools to informal and small businesses, so they can work more efficiently, look professional and grow.

Who we work with

Businesses at every stage

Retailers, food businesses, beauty businesses, clothing businesses, service providers, crafters and other growing businesses, whether you are just starting out or growing your customer base.

We also assist with business proposals for businesses that want to expand, apply for funding or partner with other businesses.
